Architecture Decision Records are valuable only when they are short, specific, and connected to operational consequences. Long narrative documents with vague conclusions are rarely revisited.

A useful ADR answers five questions clearly: context, decision, alternatives considered, trade-offs accepted, and expected operational impact.

## ADR quality criteria

- Problem statement is concrete and time-scoped.
- Decision is explicit and testable.
- Rejected alternatives include brief rationale.
- Risks and mitigations are listed.
- Follow-up review date is defined.

This structure creates decision memory that survives team changes and helps future contributors understand why a system is shaped the way it is.

## Cultural effect

Teams that maintain ADR discipline make faster design decisions later because foundational reasoning is already documented.

A strong ADR practice turns architecture from opinion into accountable engineering history.
